# Holistic Rising: Treatments & Services Knowledge Base

## Introduction

**Holistic Rising** is a sanctuary for the soul, mind, and body, founded by the mother-and-son duo **Maria Sarmiento** and **Diego Robinson**. Rooted in a long lineage of South American shamanic healers, the clinic offers a range of heartfelt, intentional treatments that blend ancient wisdom with modern therapeutic clinical expertise. With locations in London and Essex, Holistic Rising provides a non-judgmental space where clients are supported through their unique, non-linear healing journeys.

The clinic’s philosophy is centered on the principle of **Love** and the use of **Frequency Medicine**. By viewing the body as an energetic system, Holistic Rising aims to restore vibrational coherence to cells, organs, and emotions that have been disrupted by stress, trauma, or illness. Their approach is fluid and flexible, meeting each client exactly where they are—"peeling back the layers of the onion"—to address the root causes of imbalance and promote deep, lasting transformation.

What sets Holistic Rising apart is their commitment to **Heart-Led Healing** and the fusion of diverse modalities—ranging from trauma-focused physiotherapy and sports massage to Japanese Reiki and Ayurvedic rituals. Every session is designed to initiate a nervous system reset in-clinic, followed by practical aftercare tools to empower clients to maintain balance and clarity in their everyday lives.
